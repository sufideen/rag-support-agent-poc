# New Service Connections and Moving Home — GridPulse Energy

## Moving into a new property

If you're moving into a property already connected to the GridPulse network:

1. Contact us at least 48 hours before your move date, either online at gridpulse-energy.co.uk/moving or by calling 0800 555 0166.
2. Provide your move-in date, new address, and — if possible — a meter reading taken on the day you move in.
3. We'll set up a new account in your name from your move-in date. If you don't provide a reading, we'll use an estimate until one is submitted.

If you're moving out, follow the same process to close your account, providing a final meter reading and your forwarding address for the final bill.

## Connecting a property with no existing supply

New connections (for example, a new-build property or a property that has never had a GridPulse connection) require a site survey. The process is:

1. Submit a connection request via gridpulse-energy.co.uk/new-connection with the property address and required capacity (standard domestic or higher for things like EV charging or heat pumps).
2. A surveyor will assess the site, typically within 10 working days of your request.
3. You'll receive a quote covering connection costs, which vary based on distance from the nearest supply point and any groundworks required.
4. Once you accept the quote, installation is typically scheduled within 4-6 weeks, though this can extend during periods of high demand.

## Upgrading your supply capacity

If you're installing an EV charger, heat pump, or other high-load equipment, your existing connection may need a capacity upgrade. Request an assessment via the same new-connection process above — a surveyor will confirm whether your existing supply is sufficient or needs upgrading.

## Temporary connections

Temporary supplies (for events, construction sites, or similar) can be arranged separately — contact our business connections team on 0800 555 0144, as this follows a different process from domestic connections.

## Switching supplier to GridPulse

If you're moving to GridPulse from another supplier at your current address (not moving home), this is a supplier switch rather than a new connection, and no site survey or physical work is needed. Switches typically complete within 21 days and you'll be notified of your switch date in advance.
