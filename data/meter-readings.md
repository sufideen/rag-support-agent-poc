# Submitting Meter Readings — GridPulse Energy

## Why submit readings

Submitting regular meter readings ensures your bills reflect actual usage rather than estimates, which can otherwise leave you overpaying or facing a large catch-up bill later.

## How to submit a reading

1. Online via portal.gridpulse-energy.co.uk — select "Submit a Reading"
2. Via the GridPulse mobile app
3. By phone on 0800 555 0188 (automated line, available 24/7)
4. By text: text READING followed by your meter number and reading to 60555

We recommend submitting a reading on the same date each month for the most consistent billing.

## Smart meters

If you have a smart meter, readings are usually sent automatically every 30 minutes and you don't need to submit manual readings. You can check your smart meter is sending data correctly via the "Smart Meter Status" section of your online account.

If your smart meter shows as "Not Communicating," this doesn't affect your supply, only automatic reading collection. Common causes include a weak signal in your area or the meter needing a manual reset by an engineer. Report this via the portal or by calling support, and we'll investigate — this is usually resolved remotely within a few days.

## Reading a traditional (non-smart) meter

- **Digital display meters**: read the numbers left to right, ignoring any numbers shown in red or after a decimal point.
- **Dial meters**: read each dial left to right. If the pointer is between two numbers, use the lower number. If it's directly on a number, check the dial to its right — if that dial reads 0-1, use the lower number on the dial you're reading; otherwise use the higher number.

## Estimated vs actual readings

If you haven't submitted a reading in a while, your bill will be estimated based on your historical usage pattern. This is clearly marked as "Estimated" on your bill. Submitting an actual reading at any time will trigger a corrected bill on your next billing cycle, which may result in a credit or additional charge depending on whether the estimate was too high or too low.

## Moving to a smart meter

Smart meter installation is free and can be booked online or by calling 0800 555 0188. Installation takes approximately 1-2 hours and requires an adult present. Once installed, you'll no longer need to submit manual readings.
