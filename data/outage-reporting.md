# Reporting a Power Outage — GridPulse Energy

## How to report an outage

If your power has gone out, you can report it in three ways:

1. **Online**: Log into your GridPulse account at portal.gridpulse-energy.co.uk and select "Report an Outage." This is the fastest way to get an accurate restoration estimate, since the system cross-references your postcode against known faults.
2. **Phone**: Call our 24/7 outage line on 0800 555 0199. Have your account number or postcode ready.
3. **Text**: Text OUTAGE to 60555 with your postcode.

## Before you report

Check these first, as they can save you a call:

- Check if a neighbour has power. If they do, the issue may be inside your property (a tripped fuse or RCD), not a supply fault.
- Check our live outage map at gridpulse-energy.co.uk/outages, which shows known faults and estimated restoration times without needing to call.
- If you smell gas or see a fallen power line, do not attempt to investigate. Call our emergency line on 0800 111 999 immediately and stay well away from the area.

## What happens after you report

Once reported, you'll receive a text update within 15 minutes confirming the fault has been logged, along with an estimated restoration time if one is available. For widespread outages affecting more than 500 properties, updates are also posted to our outage map and social media channels.

Planned restoration times are estimates based on fault type:

- Local fuse or cable fault: typically 1-4 hours
- Substation fault: typically 4-8 hours
- Storm damage affecting overhead lines: can take 24-48 hours depending on access and weather

## Priority Services Register

If you or someone in your household depends on power for medical equipment, is elderly, or has a disability, register for our Priority Services Register. Registered customers receive priority restoration, advance warning of planned outages, and a dedicated support line. Register free at gridpulse-energy.co.uk/priority or by calling 0800 555 0199.

## Compensation for prolonged outages

Under Ofgem's Guaranteed Standards, if your power is off for more than 12 hours (or 18 hours during severe weather), you may be entitled to compensation. This is usually paid automatically to your account within 10 working days of restoration, but if you haven't received it, contact customer support with your outage reference number.
