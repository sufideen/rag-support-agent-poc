# Account and Online Portal Help — GridPulse Energy

## Logging in

Access your account at portal.gridpulse-energy.co.uk or via the GridPulse mobile app (available on iOS and Android). You'll need the email address registered on your account and your password.

## Resetting your password

1. On the login page, select "Forgot password"
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your inbox for a reset link, valid for 24 hours (check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes)
4. Choose a new password — this must be at least 10 characters and include a number and a symbol

If you no longer have access to your registered email, contact support to verify your identity and update your account details.

## Updating your details

Log in and go to "Account Settings" to update your email address, phone number, or postal address. Changing your registered email requires confirming the change via a link sent to the new address.

## Setting up online-only billing

To switch from paper bills to online-only, go to "Account Settings" → "Billing Preferences" and select "Online only." This typically takes effect from your next billing cycle.

## Adding another person to your account

To add a joint account holder (for example, a partner or housemate), both parties need to contact support together, either by phone or via a joint request through the online portal, to verify identity and consent.

## Account security

We will never ask for your full password over the phone or by email. If you receive a suspicious email claiming to be from GridPulse asking for payment details or login credentials, do not click any links — forward it to phishing@gridpulse-energy.co.uk and delete it.

## Contacting support

- General enquiries: 0800 555 0100, Monday-Friday 8am-8pm, Saturday 9am-1pm
- Live chat: available via the portal during the same hours
- Priority Services Register members: dedicated line 0800 555 0111, extended hours including Sundays

If your query isn't resolved on first contact, ask for a reference number — this lets any follow-up call pick up exactly where you left off.
