# Understanding Your Bill and Payment Options — GridPulse Energy

## Reading your bill

Your GridPulse bill shows:

- **Usage period**: the dates covered by this bill
- **Consumption**: your electricity usage in kWh, either from an actual meter reading or an estimate
- **Standing charge**: a fixed daily fee covering the cost of maintaining your connection, charged regardless of usage
- **Unit rate**: the price per kWh consumed
- **VAT**: charged at 5% on domestic energy bills

If your bill is marked "Estimated," it means we haven't received a recent meter reading and have calculated your usage based on your historical pattern. Submitting an actual reading will correct this on your next bill.

## Payment methods

- **Direct Debit**: the cheapest option, typically saving 6% compared to other payment methods, with a fixed monthly amount recalculated periodically based on your annual estimated usage.
- **Online**: pay any time at portal.gridpulse-energy.co.uk using a debit or credit card.
- **Phone**: automated payment line on 0800 555 0177, available 24/7.
- **Pay As You Go**: available for customers with a smart prepayment meter; top up online, via the GridPulse app, or at any PayPoint location.

## Setting up Direct Debit

Direct Debit can be set up online or by phone. You'll need your bank sort code and account number. Your monthly payment is calculated from your estimated annual usage divided by 12, then reviewed every 6 months and adjusted if your actual usage differs significantly from the estimate.

## If you're struggling to pay

Contact us as early as possible — options include:

- A **payment plan** spreading what you owe over an agreed period
- **Priority Services Register** support if you're vulnerable
- Referral to the **Warm Home Discount** scheme, which may provide a one-off credit to your account if you meet eligibility criteria
- Referral to independent debt advice services such as StepChange or Citizens Advice

We will never disconnect a vulnerable customer's supply for non-payment without agreeing an alternative arrangement first.

## Disputing a bill

If you believe your bill is incorrect, contact support with your account number and the billing period in question. Common causes include an estimated reading that was significantly off, or a meter fault. We aim to resolve billing queries within 10 working days.
